## About This Series

Every article in this series is written the way a senior engineer would explain a topic to a smart student the night before an exam — not by listing bullet points to memorize, but by building genuine understanding from first principles. You will not just know *what* the answer is; you will know *why* it is the answer and be able to reconstruct it from reasoning.

Each article:
- Opens with the **exact exam question** from the 2024 and/or 2025 paper
- Builds the concept from the ground up with clear analogies
- Uses **diagrams** and **comparison tables** to make structure visible
- Gives you the depth to answer a 10-mark or 4-mark question confidently
- Connects the topic to adjacent concepts so you see the full picture

## How to Use This Series for Exam Preparation

**Full preparation (one week before exam):**  
Read all 8 files in order. Each builds on the previous. Pay special attention to comparison tables — "differentiate between X and Y" is the most common question pattern.

**Targeted revision (night before exam):**  
Go directly to the topic file. The section headers map 1-to-1 with exam questions. Find your question, read the explanation, then re-read the "Exam Tip" callouts.

**Quick recall (morning of exam):**  
Jump to the "Key Takeaways" section at the bottom of each file.

## Exam Paper Structure (Both Years)

Both papers follow the same format:
- **Q1:** Attempt **any 4** out of 5 sub-questions — 20 marks total (5 marks each)
- **Q2–Q6:** Attempt **any 3** out of remaining 5 — 60 marks total (10 marks each, split as two 10-mark sub-questions)
- **Total:** 80 marks, 3 hours

> The 2024 and 2025 papers share ~60% of their topics. Mastering this series covers both papers completely.
